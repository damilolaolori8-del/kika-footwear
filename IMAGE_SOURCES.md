# Assignment imagery

Assets are bundled so the demonstration does not depend on a visitor reaching external image servers. All stock, availability and authentication/payment functionality remain demonstrative. Brand imagery is for this educational catalogue and is not evidence of affiliation, inventory or authenticity. Before commercial use, replace third-party references with owner/supplier-approved licensed imagery of actual stock.
